Usage & Dosage FAQ

How much astaxanthin per day?

Clinical studies generally use 4 mg to 12 mg daily. For general maintenance, 4mg is standard. For intense skin UV protection or joint recovery, 12 mg astaxanthin is the optimal clinical dose.

Should I take it in the morning or night?

Most users prefer morning or afternoon. Because it can slightly increase cellular energy levels, taking it right before bed might disrupt sleep for sensitive individuals.

Empty stomach or with food?

Always take it with food. Astaxanthin is a fat-soluble carotenoid. Consuming it with healthy fats (like Omega-3, avocado, or olive oil) massively increases absorption rates.

Is astaxanthin safe? What are the side effects?
