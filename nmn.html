Why You Feel Slower After 35

Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide (NAD+) is the essential coenzyme found in every living cell that powers mitochondria to produce cellular energy (ATP). By age 40, your natural NAD+ production plummets by up to 50%, leading to sluggish metabolism, longer workout recovery times, and accelerated cellular aging.

NMN vs NR: The Direct Conversion Pathway

Unlike Nicotinamide Riboside (NR) which must undergo phosphorylation inside the cell, Beta-Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (NMN) enters cells intact through dedicated cellular transporters, delivering a rapid and sustained rise in intracellular NAD+ pools.
